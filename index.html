Consultation request

Before you book.

These answers help first-time clients and serious tattoo buyers understand how the studio handles design, visible placements and healing.

Do I need a finished design before booking?

No. Send your idea, reference direction and preferred placement. The artist can help shape it into a tattoo-ready concept.

Can I book a neck or hand tattoo as a first tattoo?

It depends on your lifestyle, confidence and pain tolerance. Visible placements are discussed carefully during consultation.

Do cover-ups need a consultation?

Yes. Cover-ups require photo review because darkness, size, age and placement affect what can be done realistically.

How do I care for a new tattoo?

Keep it clean, avoid scratching, follow the artist’s aftercare instructions and protect healed tattoos from heavy sun exposure.
